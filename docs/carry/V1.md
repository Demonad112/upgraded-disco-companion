## Built
- `components/diagrams/`: `StepDiagram.tsx` (slot, lazy mount), `parts.tsx` (label, square node, hairline, arrow),
  `WaystoneLadder.tsx`, `AtlasPath.tsx`, `BossOrder.tsx`. Test: `apps/web/test/diagrams.test.ts` (keys on the right
  steps; no hex, no rounded shapes, capital labels).
- Lazy: each SVG is its own chunk (≈2.5–3 KB raw), loaded through `React.lazy` once an IntersectionObserver sees the
  slot within 200 px of the viewport. A closed `<details>` has no layout, so nothing loads until the step is opened.
  The box reserves the aspect ratio (no shift on mount). `role="img"` + `aria-label` sit on the always-rendered
  `<figure>`, so the meaning is there before the SVG loads.
- Colours are inline `var(--token)` styles, not Tailwind `fill-*`/`stroke-*` utilities: utilities went into the
  global stylesheet and pushed every route +56 B br (`/` went over 190,000 B). `next/dynamic` was swapped for
  `React.lazy` for the same reason (−1.3 KB br on `/checklist/`).
